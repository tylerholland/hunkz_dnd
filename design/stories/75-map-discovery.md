# Feature Story 75: Map Discovery (fog of war)

**Status**: Parked idea — not planned (written 2026-11-06). Worth revisiting for a campaign where the party arrives somewhere unknown (a new realm or town). The table's DM mostly runs exploration by description, so this is an optional game mode, not a default.
**Related**: Story 76 (Places and Scenes), the adventure map in the Map Library.

## The idea

The adventure map starts dark. The party sees only what it has explored, so exploring reveals the next part of the map.

- **The room you're in**: fully lit.
- **Places you've been**: stay revealed but slightly faded, a remembered map. Over a session it becomes a record of the journey.
- **Everything else**: dark.
- **Party token**: one marker for the group, not five.
- **Place markers**: appear only once their area is revealed, except **rumours**: the DM can show a "?" where the party has *heard* of something while it's still dark. That gives the party a sense of choice about where to head.

## Revealing (it must stay light for the DM)

1. **No prep needed**: when the party enters a room, the DM drags a rough box or lasso over it; it's revealed for everyone.
2. **Follows the party token**: moving the token into an area lights it; the previous one fades to "visited".
3. **Optional prep**: outline and name rooms beforehand ("Crypt entrance", "The flooded hall") so revealing is one tap and the name appears for players.
4. **The DM sees everything**, with the fog as a light veil over his map.
5. **Overland maps**: reveal a circle around the party token as it moves, instead of boxes.

## Player choice (optional)

A player taps a spot to suggest "Let's head here". A small marker shows for everyone; the DM moves the party token when the group agrees.

## With combat

A fight in a room switches to Combat as today (a closer battle map, or the same map with tokens). Coming back returns everyone to the explored map, party token where it was.

## Open questions

- Rumour markers: do players see what the rumour says, or only that one exists?
- Rooms and towns first, overland later?
