Player End Turn

You are Eòghan. Use DM: Next Turn to move initiative along and watch the End Turn button and the "Your turn" cue. On Eòghan's turn, press End Turn yourself.

What to look at
  • End Turn (in the Initiative block) is always there. It's muted on others' turns ("Liu Sha's turn"), has a light wash when "You're next", and is lit, but steady with no pulsing, on your turn ("End Turn · when you're done").
  • Your turn cue: a banner covers only the left panel for about 1.5s, then slides back (or fades with reduced motion). It plays a soft chime and the tab title becomes "● Your turn".
  • Turn alerts is a player menu switch. Off means no banner and no chime; the button still lights up.
