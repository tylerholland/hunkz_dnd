Design Notes
①Combat mode shifts layout from 2-col to 3-col. Dice roller promotes to center (1fr). Transition: 300ms ease-out.
②Active turn: left accent bar pulses (2s), card lifts 2px, name glows, all other cards dim to 0.82. Click any card header or Next Turn to advance.
③HP bar: 5 segments. Color by threshold — gem (>50%), amber #c8a040 (20–50%), red #c06060 (<20%). Damage flash 300ms; heal glow 250ms.
④NPC HP drain trail: bar drops instantly; ghost segment (rgba white) lingers and fades 400ms — fighting-game style. Bloodied at 50%, Dead at 0.
⑤Dice roll: shake 200ms → spin 600ms (per-type rotations, blur-to-sharp) → settle 200ms → result reveal. Nat20: gold burst + CRITICAL. Nat1: red flash + FUMBLE + tilt.
⑥Portraits kept visible in all states (user decision). The brief proposed collapse to 4px bar during combat — rejected.
⑦Tier 2 content (XP, coin) auto-hides during combat via max-height transition. Per-card toggle available (⋯ menu).
⑧Map panel auto-collapses when combat begins. Map library strip hides entirely. Both restore when combat ends.
⑨Party-wide actions strip collapses to ⋯ Party button in top bar during combat. Session management actions are never needed mid-round.
⑩Death saves row hidden until HP hits 0. Click + on HP bar to reach 0 and reveal it. Three failures → FALLEN state (card dims, name strikethrough).
