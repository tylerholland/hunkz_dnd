Section 5A of 7 — Central Practical Concern
Action Memory And Turn Cadence
A 300–500ms burst is correct animation design; it may still fail as table communication when a turn takes 60–120 seconds and the watcher glanced at their phone during the first second. This section compares candidate persistence strategies rather than assuming Phase A's burst is sufficient — it deliberately does not pick a winner for you.
Case: Damage Dealt To An NPC (the only case with a real "did I miss it?" risk)
A — Lingering Residue Only
Recommended default
Story 52's own Phase B: the wound halo, breathing quietly at 0.5↔0.85 opacity, no re-announcement. This is what ships today per the brief.
B — Lingering Source/Target Memory Mark
Comparison candidate — not in any approved brief
A faint dotted connector between attacker and target, in addition to the wound halo, fading out by "until next action." Answers "who did this" as well as "who's hurt" — at the cost of a second persistent element and a second thing to fade correctly.
C — Quiet Reminder Pulse / Replay
Comparison candidate — OFF by default
A single soft re-pulse of the wound halo partway through the window (here, at the 5s mark only — not looping), or an explicit manual "↻" replay affordance on the token/detail-card. Included for comparison per the brief's instruction to treat periodic re-pulsing as a candidate, not the default answer.
Cases That Need No Persistence Strategy At All
Story 53 conditions are already Tier 2, ambient, with no resting animation and no fade — a badge applied while a viewer is looking away is still there at 5s, 10s, and "until next action," unchanged, whether it was applied to the acting token (self) or a different one. There is nothing lossy about a condition badge the way there is about a 450ms Phase A burst — the memory problem is specific to damage flash and, per below, to the attack tracer.
Concurrent Case — Damage + Self Condition + Other-Token Condition, Same Short Span
Aragorn's strike wounds and poisons Goblin 1 while Eoghan's bolt frightens the Bandit Captain, seconds apart. Neither Phase B halo nor either badge column is disturbed by the other event settling in — this is composition Rule 3/6 doing its job over table time, not just over 500ms.
Recommendation carried from the brief — flagged for sign-off

Prefer Strategy A (calm lingering residue) as the shipped default. It survives a scan-the-board glance, costs nothing extra in the layer budget, and is what Story 52 already specifies as Phase B.

Strategy B (source/target memory mark) is the strongest upgrade candidate if playtesting shows "who did this" gets lost — it is not in any of the four approved briefs and would need its own design pass before being treated as more than a comparison sketch.

Strategy C (reminder pulse / replay) is deliberately shown off by default here — the brief's explicit instruction is to treat periodic re-pulsing as a candidate to weigh, not an assumed answer, because a board that keeps re-announcing old news is the "constant alert surface" the brief is trying to avoid.
