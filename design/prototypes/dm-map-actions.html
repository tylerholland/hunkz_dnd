Take Action from the Map

Long-press a token to act with it, then pick its target: long-press another token, or the same one to target itself. Choose an action, roll or type the damage, and apply it. The roll log gets a Deal Damage button on players' targeted damage rolls.

    What changes in the app

    • The old long-press menu (Resize / Remove / Cancel) goes away. Resize stays on the hover card.
    • Tapping and dragging a token to move it works exactly as today.
    • The animation plays exactly the chosen action on every screen, players' included. The app stops guessing from the roll history.
    • Plain −/+ HP edits still animate, using the old guess.
