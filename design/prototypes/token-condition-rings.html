Condition Rings

A ring around any token with a condition or spell effect, so everyone knows to hover or tap it. Switch styles below; hover or tap a token to see what's on it.

What each style says

  • A: one question answered at a glance: is something bad (rune ring) or good (warm halo) happening to them?
  • B: how many conditions and which kind, without opening anything.
  • C: A's rings, plus spell effects that are recognisable on sight (the Hex sigil you liked, Bless motes).

Colours

Condition families as today: control (stunned, paralyzed…) pink, bind (prone, restrained, grappled) amber, sense (blinded, charmed, frightened, deafened) violet, physical (poisoned, exhaustion) green. Helpful effects are gold. Spell sigils take the caster's colour.

What changes

The small corner badges go away in favour of the ring. Invisible keeps its own veil treatment. Rings grow with the token (the Carrion's is huge) and never get too small to notice when zoomed out.
