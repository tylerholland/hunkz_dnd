Prototype · party showcase experiments

Orbs on pedestals

Each portrait is the skin of a glowing orb that turns (sways like a globe) and bobs above a slowly rotating pedestal. The pedestal's four faces carry the name, the epithet, race · class · level, and HP. Hover an orb to quicken it.

Tarot

The party as a tarot spread: gilt frames, an arched portrait, a numeral and a title banner, with a slow foil shimmer. Hover a card to turn it over for the stats.

Campfire circle

The party seated round a fire, seen at an angle: medallions on an ellipse, warmed on the side facing the flames, sparks rising. Those nearer the fire's front sit larger. Hover someone to lift them and show their HP.
