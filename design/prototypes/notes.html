Rev 2 — Interaction annotations
1 Collapsed notes strip sits below the Damage/Heal footer. No notes: faint with pencil icon + "Note" prompt. The Damage/Heal buttons are untouched.
2 When notes exist: strip gets accent-tinted background + count badge. Visual weight communicates "something is here" without rendering text.
3 Tap anywhere on the strip to expand in-place. The card grows downward — no modal. Input auto-focuses so the DM can type immediately.
4 Delete × requires no confirmation. Note fades out. Badge and strip state update instantly.
5 Shared player notes appear inside the same expanded panel, below a gem-colored divider. Read-only, italic, left-border gem accent.
6 Active-turn card glows with character's palette accent — same behavior in real app.
7 Player notes on Combat tab — below weapons quick-ref, above dice roller. Share toggle: empty dot = Private, filled gem dot = Shared with DM.
8 NPC notes use the same strip pattern. "Session only" hint reinforces that notes are discarded when combat ends.
Design decision: notes-as-strip, below footer The Damage/Heal buttons occupy the card footer entirely — they are the DM's most-used in-combat actions and must stay dominant and uncluttered. Adding a Notes button alongside them would shrink all three buttons below comfortable tap size. Instead, a thin collapsible strip appears as its own distinct row below the footer, similar to how a "drawer" attaches below the card. The strip is always present (discoverable) but visually subordinate (low height, muted style when empty). This satisfies the product owner's "don't clutter the card" constraint while keeping notes in-context and zero extra taps away.
