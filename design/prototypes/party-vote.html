Party vote

The DM asks the table a quick question with a few options. Each player's page shows the vote; they pick and send; the DM sees the tally build and the outcome. Try it: Call a Vote, then vote as Eòghan on his page below (desktop or phone). The rest of the party votes on their own. The DM chooses whether players see the votes as they come in, and whether they see the result.
