The Party

At rest each character floats as a glass orb with a nameplate (and, optionally, a sliver of HP). Hover (or tap) and the orb's rim widens into the card's border: the circular mask morphs into the rounded card, the glass shading fades, the picture settles flat, and the name, HP and links rise in.
