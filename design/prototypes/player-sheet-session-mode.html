Eoghan never intended to make a pact. The sword appeared on the night his unit was slaughtered — an obsidian blade jutting from the mud, humming with a warmth that shouldn't have been there. He picked it up to survive. The Hexblade accepted the deal without words.

He tells himself he controls it. The blade tells him nothing at all.

① Single surface
Sections divided by 1px rules + IM Fell English labels — not floating card widgets. Only the HP hero, dice roller, and modals get card elevation.
② Left column wash
linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(accent,.05), rgba(accent,.02), transparent) — tint to distinguish identity/reference side from action right column.
③ Avatar grow + ring glow
44px resting → 56px active turn (party). Own portrait 56px → 72px on own turn. 220ms ease-out scale; 1.8s ownGlowPulse/partyGlowPulse.
④ Two-step Attack → Damage
Damage button dim at rest. After Attack click, brightens (dmgBtnBrighten 240ms) then holds hot style for 8s, then decays 320ms ease-out.
⑤ Per-row adv/dis chip
24×24px pill. Tap cycles · → ▲ → ▼ → · . Resets to · after next Attack roll (advChipReset 180ms scale bounce).
⑥ Slot auto-consume
Highest available slot of spell level decremented on Cast. Pip scales 1→.7→1 (slotPip 220ms). Undo link in history (8s then fades). No slot = buttons disabled, labeled "No L2 slot".
⑦ Mobile: init collapsed
Collapses to 1 active-turn line + ▼ Show order. max-height 0→400px, 220ms cubic-bezier(.34,1,.64,1). Arrow rotates 180°. Always expanded on desktop.
⑧ Auto-switch logic
If entries.length > 0 AND round > 0 AND no stored preference → opens in Session. Toggle receives 400ms accentPulse border hint. Explicit player override wins.
⑨ DieShape SVG polygons
d4 triangle · d6 square · d8 diamond · d10 pentagon · d12 heptagon · d20 near-circle. stroke=#6a8fa8 (d4-d12), stroke=#8bb4cc (d20). No border-box buttons.
⑩ Roll history
All rolls (weapon rows, ability chips, dice panel) write to shared history. Source label in IM Fell English 10px muted. Newest entry gets 220ms border pulse.
