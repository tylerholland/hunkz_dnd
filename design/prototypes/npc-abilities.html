One list: abilities & defense

Prototype. The creature's free-text Abilities and its Attacks become one list in one editor. Any ability can be switched to “⚔ Can be rolled”, which gives it the attack roll, damage and look; plain ones stay as traits and notes. The card shows rollable abilities with their dice, then the rest. Edit the list on the left and watch the card and Take Action follow.
