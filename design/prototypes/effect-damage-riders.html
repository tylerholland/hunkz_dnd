Effect Damage Riders

The Carrion Crawler is ✦ Hexed by Eòghan. Hex adds +1d6 necrotic to Eòghan's hits on it, and nobody else's. Two ways the app could carry that +1d6 so nobody has to remember it. Try both, in both palettes.

Compare
  • A · Attack bar adds it: the player's DAMAGE step shows a "✦ +1d6 Hex" chip, on by default, and rolls it in. The log shows the split, and Deal Damage applies the total.
  • B · DM log offers it: the player rolls damage as now, and the DM's log row shows "✦ Hexed by Eòghan: +1d6 necrotic" with a Roll +1d6 button before Deal Damage.
  • Liu Sha's hit (bottom of each log) shows no Hex prompt, because Hex only adds to the caster's hits.
