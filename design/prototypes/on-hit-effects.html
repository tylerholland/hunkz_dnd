On-hit effects

Exploratory prototype. An attack can carry an optional On hit effect: a condition (Poisoned, Charmed, Paralyzed…) or one of your effects (Hexed, Baned, your custom ones), with an optional saving throw. Set one up on the left, then play the attack on the right and watch what it adds to the Deal Damage step.
