Token Tray Parity

The DM's battle view. Hover anything: a token, a tray chip, or a card. Tokens and cards light each other up, and the tray behaves like the map.

What changed
  • NPC portraits in the tray: the Goblin Scout and Ogre in the tray show their portraits, not initials, the same as on the map.
  • Tray hover card: hovering a tray chip shows the same card as a map token, opening upward.
  • Token → card: hovering a token or chip outlines its card with a glow in the token's colour, on top of any active-turn styling (Eòghan's card is the active turn).
  • Card → token: hovering a card pulses a ring on its token, or on its tray chip if it isn't placed yet.
  • No scrolling: a highlighted card that's scrolled out of view stays put.
