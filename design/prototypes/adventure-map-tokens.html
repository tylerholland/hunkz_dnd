Adventure map tokens

The party token sits on the city map, and the DM drags it wherever he says the party is. When the party splits, he drags characters from the party list onto the map. He adds markers on demand (landmarks everyone sees, or hidden pins only he sees). Drag anything onto “Remove from map” to take it off. Players see the same map, minus hidden pins.
