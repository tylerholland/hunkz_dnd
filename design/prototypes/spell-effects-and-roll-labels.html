Spell Effects & Roll Labels

Prototype for four connected changes. Everything is clickable, and the event log in section 3 collects what you do in sections 2 and 4, as the real app's shared log does.

Scenarios
  • Hex without damage: Eòghan casts Hex on the Carrion Crawler. The cast is rolled. On success the player sends ✦ Effect. The DM applies it with a note on what it does. The token gets the hex sigil and the cast animation plays.
  • Free roll with a reason: a player types what a free roll is for. It becomes the log label and the field clears.
  • DM relabels afterwards: the DM adds a short label to any roll from the ✎ on the log row.
Open questions
  • Should a failed cast still offer ✦ Effect? Here it's always offered and the table decides, matching how Deal Damage works today.
  • Should a player be able to remove an effect they cast (end the Hex), or only the DM? Here, only the DM (via the existing condition menu).
  • Should the DM's labels also apply to rows in the Chronicle archive, or only to the live log?

1 · Spell setup (Edit Character → Spells)

One new control: Effect. Damage stays optional, so a spell can have damage, an effect, or both. Try clearing the effect or adding damage to see how the attack bar below changes.

2 · Player attack bar (Session view, phone width)

Session map: Eòghan has targeted the Carrion Crawler

For spells, ATTACK reads CAST, and it always rolls (your table rolls to see if spells succeed). Afterwards the bar offers ✦ EFFECT if the spell has one and ⚔ DAMAGE if it has damage dice. Sending the effect leaves a calm "sent" state; the DM decides.

3 · Event log & Apply Effect

Roll LogDM view

The DM gets [Apply Effect] on effect rows, styled like Deal Damage, and a faint ✎ on hover to label any roll. Players see the same rows without either.

4 · Free roll description

Free Roll

The description sits right above Roll, so it's easy to fill in but never required. Its text becomes the log label (Free Roll · Climbing the grate), and it clears after every roll. Enter in the field also rolls.

The DM's ✎ (section 3) covers rolls a player didn't label: click it, type, and press Enter to save or Esc to cancel. The label shows for everyone and feeds the Chronicle timeline.
